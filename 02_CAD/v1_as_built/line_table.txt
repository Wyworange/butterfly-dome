LINE TABLE - which cam / rocker drives which butterfly (v1 design)

This table is the full original design: 12 butterflies, each with a 'bob' line (the butterfly
moves up/down on its slider) and a 'flap' line (the wings close).

AS BUILT (final piece): flap only, 4 large + 3 medium butterflies.
  Used rockers : 2, 4, 6, 8 (large #1-#4, 6.3 mm pull) and 9, 10 (medium, 3.15 mm pull + 0.4 mm margin)
  Not used     : bob rockers 1, 3, 5, 7, 11, 12 (left empty or fitted with filler)
  Sleeves      : sliders glued fixed in the sleeves, so the butterflies do not bob.
  Mediums      : of the 8 small positions below, only 3 were fitted: two on rocker 9 (Small A),
                 one on rocker 10 (Small B). In SYSTEM.md they are numbered #5-#7.
